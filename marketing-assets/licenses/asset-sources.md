# Asset sources and provenance

## Product screenshots

- Source: local VinOS application in `D:\cellarflow`.
- Account: authorized `testuser1` demo workspace.
- Capture date: 2026-07-14.
- Capture method: authenticated local app session in the Codex in-app browser.
- Data status: fictional seeded demonstration data.
- Processing: browser capture bytes were normalized to true PNG, then used in cropped safe-frame contact sheets and videos.

## Campaign photography

The following images were created with OpenAI’s built-in image-generation tool for this project. No third-party stock photographs were used.

- `photos/01-kakheti-vineyard-operations-hero.png` — Kakheti vineyard manager using a tablet at sunrise.
- `photos/02-qvevri-cellar-operations-hero.png` — Georgian qvevri cellar with a winemaker using a tablet.
- `photos/03-modern-cellar-lab-operations-hero.png` — winery lab/cellar sampling scene.

The final prompts required photorealistic, language-neutral editorial campaign imagery with no logos, text, watermarks, wine-tasting props, or visible software interface. These are concept images and should pass the publisher’s normal brand, legal, and regional-authenticity review before paid publication.

## Derived assets

- Product contact sheets, posters, and MP4 tours were generated locally from the captured screenshots.
- MP4 files are silent H.264/YUV420p exports with fast-start metadata.
- Copy and voiceover scripts were authored from repository-evidenced capabilities and reviewed to avoid unsupported performance, regulatory, localization, IoT, and AI claims.
