# VinOS marketing copy — English

## Positioning

VinOS is a connected operating workspace for wineries. It brings vineyard work, cellar production, traceability, documentation, and business control into one connected workspace, with English and Georgian interface options.

## Hero

**Headline:** From vineyard to bottle—one system.

**Subhead:** Plan vineyard work, coordinate cellar operations, follow each recorded lot, and keep the team aligned in one connected workspace.

**Primary CTA:** Book a VinOS walkthrough

**Secondary CTA:** See the product tour

## Six feature benefits

1. **See every block clearly** — Track location records, phenology, rule-based weather-risk indicators, scouting, spray records, maturity sampling, and harvest.
2. **Coordinate cellar work** — Manage grape intake, wine lots, vessel capacity, qvevri passports, transfers, fermentation, lab work, and bottling.
3. **Trace each recorded lot** — Connect available vineyard, intake, lot, cellar-operation, bottling, storage, and dispatch records in one history.
4. **Turn data into next actions** — Dashboards, alerts, and tasks—and, when Gemini is configured, human-reviewed AI draft actions—keep the team focused on what needs attention.
5. **Stay ready for paperwork** — Run pre-export checks and export supported Georgian forms from available records, manage certification stages, and retain a tamper-evident audit history.
6. **Keep field work moving** — In a previously loaded workspace, queue supported operational changes locally during a connection loss, then sync and review conflicts after reconnection.

## Target users

- Winery owners and production managers
- Winemakers and cellar teams
- Viticulturists and agronomists
- Laboratory, quality, and compliance teams
- Inventory, costing, sales, and operations staff
