# VinOS marketing facts and claim guardrails

## Safe core position

**English:** One operating record from vineyard to bottle.

**ქართული:** ვენახიდან ბოთლამდე — ერთი დაკავშირებული საოპერაციო ჩანაწერი.

## Supported product capabilities

- Connected vineyard, harvest, grape intake, wine-lot, cellar-operation, lab, bottling, storage, sales, and costing workflows.
- Vineyard block registry, phenology, spray and scouting logs, sampling, harvest planning, Open-Meteo weather, GDD, and rule-based risk indicators.
- Tanks, qvevri, barrels, transfers and losses, recorded fermentation curves, lab panels, inventory, and bottling records.
- Lot lineage, printable lot passports, QR labels, vessel QR sheets, storage reservations, and dispatch records.
- Twenty Georgian Annex templates with validation and XLSX/print-ready output; fields populate only where source records are supported, and unsupported fields remain visibly blank/TODO.
- English and Georgian interface options, six role-based access profiles, an installable PWA shell, local mutation queue, sync status, and conflict review.
- When Gemini is configured, the contextual assistant can derive review-only task, lab, SO₂, spray, compliance, document, and passport draft actions from assistant responses.
- New audit entries are server-stamped into a tamper-evident SHA-256 hash-linked chain; legacy records are checked with a computed chain.

## Claims to avoid or qualify

- Do not claim live IoT telemetry or remote equipment control. Weather comes from a forecast-grid API; vessel readings are recorded operational states.
- Do not claim satellite monitoring or remote sensing.
- Do not claim complete bilingual localization; use “English and Georgian interface options.”
- Do not claim regulator approval, guaranteed compliance, or automatic e-filing.
- Do not claim that AI automatically changes records or applies treatments. AI drafts require human review.
- Do not claim scientifically validated disease prediction. Risk indicators are rule-based decision support.
- Do not claim the entire product works offline. AI, weather, maps, and integrations require connectivity/configuration.
- Do not claim blockchain or absolute immutability. Use “tamper-evident SHA-256 hash-linked audit chain.”
- Do not publish ROI, savings, uptime, customer-count, scale, storage, or security-certification claims without separate evidence.
