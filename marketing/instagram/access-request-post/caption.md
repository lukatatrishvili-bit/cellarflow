# VinOS access-request post

## English

Ready to bring your winery into VinOS? Here’s how to request access. 🍇

1. Go to vinos.ge and choose **Try now**, then **Create a workspace**.
2. Continue with Google or enter your name, work email, estate or company, reachable phone number, and password.
3. Submit the request and verify your email.
4. Every registration is reviewed manually. We’ll email you after the decision, and sign-in unlocks once your request is approved.

Accurate details help us review your request quickly. Your operational data stays private to your workspace.

→ Request access at vinos.ge
